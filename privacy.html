Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit the TDC application form, we collect the information you enter. This may include your player tag, email address, platform, preferred position, build and play style, usual play times, communication preferences, social handle, and your written answers. Please do not submit passwords or other sensitive account information.

How we use your information

TDC uses the information you provide to review your application, learn about your play preferences, and contact you about joining the crew.

Form processing and delivery

Our application form is processed by Static Forms. Submissions are delivered to the recipient inbox configured for the TDC Static Forms account. Static Forms processes submitted information to provide its service, subject to its own privacy terms.

Questions or requests

To ask a question about information you submitted or request its deletion, contact TDC at duhtdc@outlook.com.

Please do not submit passwords or other sensitive account information through the application form.
